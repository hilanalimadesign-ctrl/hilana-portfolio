About

Hi! I’m Hilana, but you can call me Lana :)

I’ve been a Product Designer for 4+ years, and I love turning complex processes into simple, intuitive and meaningful experiences. Today I’m at Esparta, where I’m the only designer on ScoreHub, a credit and digital signature platform. Before that, I worked at Viitra on the redesign of an app for Brazil’s Ministry of Education, and at Tempest on cybersecurity products.

Day to day, I go from user research to the final interface, always balancing aesthetics, usability and accessibility. I also use AI throughout the process, from research to prototype.

Beyond design, I’m also a digital illustrator and the founder of Caçadoras de Hobbies, a creative community based in Recife. Outside of work, I'm a little wanderlust-driven (I love exploring new places, meeting people, and experiencing different cultures), you’ll probably find me reading on my Kindle or exploring the world of code — this website was born from that curiosity 💻✨
